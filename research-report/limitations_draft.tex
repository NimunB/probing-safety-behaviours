\subsection{Limitations}

\textbf{The sycophancy probes are not checked against an echo shortcut.}
A sycophantic and a genuine agreement both echo the user, so a probe
reading surface agreement could not separate them. We should measure each
probe's AUROC on sycophantic against genuine agreements. Without this, the sycophancy AUROCs may partly measure the
echo.

\textbf{The judge is unvalidated.} Behaviour and coherence scores come from
a single LLM judge (Claude Haiku 4.5) whose agreement with human raters was
not measured.

\textbf{No last-pooled direction is steered.} The steering grid contains
mass-mean and logistic regression fit on mean-pooled activations and the
attention direction, but not the last-pooled probes that detect sycophancy
best. The sycophancy steering inversion may therefore partly reflect which
directions were available rather than the estimators.

\textbf{Both layer selections are anchored on mass-mean.} The detection
layer is the argmax of the mass-mean probe's cross-validated AUROC, and the
steering block is the window of maximum mass-mean NIE. Logistic regression
and the attention probe are therefore evaluated at a layer and a block
chosen for mass-mean, a mild bias toward it on both sides of the
comparison.
